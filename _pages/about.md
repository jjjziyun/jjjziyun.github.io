I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Foreign Languages and Cultures, Nanjing Normal University. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Linguistics from Zhejiang University.

My research interests include:

- **Language testing and assessment**
- **Test taker characteristics**
- **Working memory and writing**
- **AI feedback**
- **Integrated writing tasks**

我是南京师范大学外国语学院讲师，博士毕业于浙江大学外国语言学及应用语言学专业。主要研究方向为语言测试与评价、考生个体差异、工作记忆与写作、人工智能反馈和综合写作任务。
